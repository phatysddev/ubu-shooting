# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Three.js, confirmed by the user. Node.js and WebSocket server are implementation choices for online rooms.

## Product Purpose
A first-person shooter playable online in a web browser, emphasizing realistic atmosphere in Africa.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Confirmed: first-person shooting, browser play, online play, Three.js. Inferred scope for the first playable version: room-based multiplayer, fictional savanna outpost, desktop mouse and keyboard, procedural assets, a practice mode when alone. Online multiplayer means a running shared server; public hosting is not yet configured.

## Brand Commitments
Realistic atmosphere, African setting. No real conflict or faction was specified. Working title: RED EARTH (proposed).

## Evidence on Hand
Empty game repository; no supplied models, audio, or textures. Realism is an art-direction goal rather than a claim of photorealistic assets.


## Confirmed expansion
The user requested many varied guns, a knife, grenades and female characters with a strongly sexy style. Implemented as 11 guns with distinct gameplay, two equipped gun slots plus a knife, server-simulated grenades, and three explicitly adult female operators with fitted fashion-combat outfits. Procedural character models are stylized; all operators share identical gameplay hitboxes and movement for balance.


## Tactical expansion
Confirmed request: tactical abilities inspired by Valorant, jumping, improved scopes, and no knife aiming. Implemented shared dash/smoke/heal abilities with server-controlled cooldowns, synchronized jumping, dedicated sniper/DMR optical reticles, and a shared knife aim eligibility rule. No character-specific ultimate, economy, or copied Valorant assets.


## Defensive expansion
User requested adjustable mouse speed, a Free Fire-inspired ice wall, RPG, stronger kill feedback and a more durable character. Implemented independent persisted look/ADS sliders, original procedural destructible ice cover, server-simulated rockets, authoritative kill banners, and BASTION with 175 HP balanced by 78% movement speed. Other operators retain 100 HP. Existing claims about all operators sharing movement now have this explicit heavy-operator exception.


## Latest requested balance
User requested deliberately overpowered RPG, stronger explosion effects, mechanical reload animation, wheel weapon switching, and character ultimates earned by 5 kills. Trailing '4' was interpreted and disclosed as ice wall cooldown of 4 seconds. Latest RPG and ultimate values are documented in README's Overpowered update; earlier balance entries are historical. Ultimate charges count only actual player eliminations and persist through death until spent.


## Current loadout and combat expansion
The user reduced carried abilities to two selected in the lobby, requested easier aiming, better gun audio, hurt sounds, and extremely strong per-character ultimates including a map-wide explosion. Follow-up added random weapon perks, homing RPG and drivable shoot-from vehicles. Current behavior is documented in README, superseding earlier balance records. Server enforces two chosen skills, authoritative perk rolls and exclusive vehicle drivers. Four procedural single-seat jeeps respawn after destruction. No multiplayer public deployment has been configured.
